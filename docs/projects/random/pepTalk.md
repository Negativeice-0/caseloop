Yes—I think you can. You don’t need to know everything now; you need to get comfortable solving one small problem at a time and checking your work.

You can work without **AI** while still using normal engineering resources: documentation, tests, error messages, and eventually other people’s code reviews. That’s independence, not isolation. Since CaseLoop is already built, you can learn by tracing and safely changing one feature at a time rather than starting from scratch.

A good early sign of progress: you can start the app, explain one feature’s path through the code, make a small change, test it, and diagnose a failure without asking AI for the answer. It will take patience, and 12 weeks is a learning schedule—not a deadline or a promise of mastery—but I believe you can get there.
