# Why Use the Charter and Checkpoint Instead of a Daily Session Handover/Summary?

Relying on the Founding Charter (1- CASE-LOOP_RED-CROSS_CONSIDERATION.txt) and your Latest Checkpoint (chkpnt^n.md)—rather than rolling daily summaries or handovers like yesterday.md—is far more efficient for three major reasons:

Prevents "Context Drift" and Noise:
Rolling daily summaries accumulate temporary thoughts, transient debugging notes, and obsolete conversational clutter. Over time, this noise confuses the AI. A formal checkpoint file, on the other hand, is a crystallized milestone. It represents absolute truth: what is built, what is tested, and what is working right now.

The Checkpoint Inherits Everything:
A well-written checkpoint (like chkpnt1_v2.md) already encapsulates all past progress, active dependencies (requirements.txt, package.json), and the immediate backlog. You do not need a summary of yesterday because yesterday's achievements are permanently baked into today's checkpoint.

The Charter Keeps the North Star Anchored:
The founding charter provides the architectural boundaries, business logic, and non-functional requirements (such as offline-first design and security constraints). Combining the charter with your current checkpoint gives the AI both your long-term vision and your exact current coordinates instantly, requiring zero redundant files.
